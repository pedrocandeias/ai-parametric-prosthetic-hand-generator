Help & Tips

Hand Fab turns patient measurements into a 3D-printable prosthetic. Follow these steps to design and export a hand.

  1. 1 Pick a modelChoose a prosthetic hand family from the dashboard to open it in the configurator.
  2. 2 Adjust parametersEdit dimensions in the side panel. Hover the ⓘ next to any field for what it measures and its typical range.
  3. 3 Use the AI assistantDescribe the patient in plain words and let the AI suggest a starting size — then refine it manually.
  4. 4 Import measurementsLoad an anthropometric profile to auto-fill matching fields from a patient's recorded measurements.
  5. 5 Render & exportPreview the result in the 3D viewer, then export an STL for slicing and printing.
  6. 6 Save your workStore a configuration to reload it later or share it with an assigned technician.
More help

  • Lengths are anatomical (knuckle crease to fingertip), measured in millimetres:

    • Palm breadth/length/thickness — knuckle-to-knuckle width, wrist-to-MCP length, and palm-to-back thickness.
    • Finger lengths — MCP crease to tip for each digit and the thumb.
    • Gauntlet width — forearm socket width ≈ wrist circumference ÷ π, plus clearance.
    • Drag in the viewer to rotate the model.
    • Scroll to zoom; right-drag to pan.
    • Press Esc to close this panel and other menus.
